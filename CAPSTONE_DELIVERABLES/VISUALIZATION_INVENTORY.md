## What Each Chart Shows

### **01_basket_comparison.png**
- **Visual Type:** Grouped column chart
- **Data:** Monthly costs in EUR
- **Categories:** Official (€69), Real (€304), Healthy (€384)
- **Key Message:** "Official stats underestimate by 342%"
- **Presentation Time:** 1 minute

### **02_affordability_cliffs.png**
- **Visual Type:** Bar chart with color zones
- **Data:** % of monthly income
- **Categories:** SMIC, Low Income, Median, Upper Middle
- **Color Zones:** Green (excellent), Yellow (manageable), Red (crisis)
- **Key Message:** "Below €35k, families face impossible choices"
- **Presentation Time:** 1.5 minutes

### **03_gap_analysis.png**
- **Visual Type:** Waterfall or stacked
- **Data:** Building blocks of the gap
- **Shows:** Official + Missing Gap + Healthy Premium
- **Key Message:** "What's not counted"
- **Presentation Time:** 30 seconds

### **04_basket_composition.png**
- **Visual Type:** Three pie charts
- **Data:** Item counts and percentages
- **Categories:** 13 items (official), 34 items (real), 41 items (healthy)
- **Key Message:** "Real baskets are 2.6x larger"
- **Presentation Time:** 30 seconds

### **05_healthy_cliff.png**
- **Visual Type:** Bar chart
- **Data:** Remaining annual budget
- **Shows:** After food costs at different income levels
- **Key Message:** "Health inequality is price-enforced"
- **Presentation Time:** 1 minute

### **06_family_squeeze.png** ⭐
- **Visual Type:** Clustered horizontal bars
- **Data:** Annual remaining budgets for SMIC and Median families
- **Shows:** Base → +Baby → +Teenager progression
- **Key Message:** "Same cost increase, different percentage impact"
- **Annotation:** Explains why margin matters (not just %)
- **Presentation Time:** 2 minutes

### **07_housing_burden.png** ⭐
- **Visual Type:** Whisker/range chart
- **Data:** Housing costs by income (€/month and % of income)
- **Shows:** SMIC €600-850/month, Median €1,000-1,300/month
- **Key Message:** "Housing costs compress remaining budget"
- **Annotation:** Shows structural constraint
- **Presentation Time:** 1.5 minutes

### **08_complete_budget_reality.png** ⭐⭐ **CRITICAL**
- **Visual Type:** Horizontal stacked bar charts
- **Data:** All 12 essential cost categories
- **Shows:** SMIC vs Median family complete monthly budget
- **SMIC Result:** €1,824 costs vs €1,750 income = **-€74 DEFICIT**
- **Median Result:** €2,249 costs vs €3,500 income = **+€1,251 SURPLUS**
- **Key Message:** "Income is structurally insufficient; government transfers are survival requirement"
- **Annotation:** Shows why SMIC families cannot absorb composition changes
- **Presentation Time:** 2.5-3 minutes
