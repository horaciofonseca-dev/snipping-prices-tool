## Executive Summary

Snippet Tool operates in a **€2-5B total addressable market** (pricing intelligence + market data), with a realistically capturable **€50-200M market** over 5-10 years.

**Key Finding**: Multiple revenue streams (SaaS, data licensing, consulting) enable €100K-5M+ annual revenue depending on execution and market penetration.

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) & Lifetime Value (LTV)

### CAC Assumptions
- Sales & marketing: 20% of revenue (typical for B2B SaaS)
- CAC payback period: 6-9 months (healthy)

**CAC by Segment**:
- Starter customers: €500-1,000 CAC (organic + content marketing)
- Professional: €2,000-5,000 CAC (sales + partnerships)
- Enterprise: €10,000-50,000 CAC (sales + events)

### LTV Assumptions
- Gross margin: 75% (software scales well)
- Churn rate: 5-10% monthly (60-70% annual retention)
- Average contract value: €1,500-3,000/year

**LTV Examples**:
- Starter (€100/month, 3-year lifetime): €3,600 LTV / €500 CAC = 7.2x (excellent)
- Professional (€500/month, 4-year lifetime): €24,000 LTV / €3,000 CAC = 8x
- Enterprise (€3,000/month, 5-year lifetime): €180,000 LTV / €25,000 CAC = 7.2x

**Unit Economics**: Healthy LTV:CAC ratios (>3x) across all segments
